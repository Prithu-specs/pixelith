# Pixelith — Local AI Upscaling

Upscale photos and video on your own computer, up to 8K, while your phone acts
as a private local companion. Pixelith does not upload your media to a cloud AI
service and does not require an account, API key, or subscription.

## Highlights

- Local image and video processing
- Temporary secure desktop pairing—no Pixelith account
- Automatic desktop discovery on trusted Wi-Fi
- Preserve source FPS or deliberately choose 24, 30, 60, or 120 FPS
- Resolution, aspect-ratio, compression, time, and size controls
- CPU-first operation with available GPU/NPU acceleration
- Resumable segmented video processing and clear cancellation

Requires the Pixelith desktop app on Windows, macOS, or Linux. Performance and
8K feasibility depend on source length, model, and desktop hardware.
